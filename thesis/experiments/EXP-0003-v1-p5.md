# EXP-0003 — V1 P5 LiteViT

## Objective

Test whether one identity-initialized LiteViT block after the YOLOv8n P5/SPPF layer,
before PAN/FPN, improves adverse-weather detection over the `EXP-0001` baseline.
This is the first ViT ablation in `ref/idea.md`, not the full LiteViT + weather-gate
model.

## Scope

- Backbone, neck, and head are unchanged.
- One channel-preserving LiteViT block at P5 (20x20 tokens for a 640 input).
- P3/P4 remain CNN-only.
- No weather gate in V1.
- Same training and evaluation protocol as `EXP-0001`.

## Hypothesis and checks

- Initial model outputs should equal the baseline because the block is
  identity-initialized.
- Training should be stable at batch 4 with AMP; record VRAM.
- Compare val mAP50/mAP50-95 and per-condition, per-class, and per-size results
  against `EXP-0001`.
- Treat test as a secondary estimate; the test split has already been used by the
  Stage 2A baseline and ablation.

## Pre-run checks

- Model builds and the wrapped P5 layer is a `FeatureWithBlock`.
- Initial detector output matches the baseline (identity-initialized block).
- Save/load through Ultralytics preserves the custom wrapper.
- 640x640, batch 4 forward/backward smoke: **862.3 MB** PyTorch peak allocated,
  **1032.0 MB** reserved. This excludes optimizer states; full training VRAM is
  recorded by the run.
